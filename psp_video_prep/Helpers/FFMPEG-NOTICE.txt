PSP Video Prep includes FFmpeg
==============================

To read video formats that macOS cannot open by itself (MKV, WebM, AVI/DivX,
WMV, FLV and others), this app runs the "ffmpeg" program found next to this
file. It is a separate executable: no FFmpeg code is linked into the app.

The bundled copy is built from that unmodified source by the script
scripts/build-ffmpeg.sh in this app's source repository, which records the
exact configuration. The build enables no GPL or non-free components and uses
no external libraries beyond those that ship with macOS. You may replace the
bundled "ffmpeg" with your own build.
